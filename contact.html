Frequently Asked Questions

Is StreetKart really free?

Yes! StreetKart is completely free for vendors. Our mission is to provide digital visibility to all local vendors, regardless of their ability to pay.

Do you handle deliveries?

No, StreetKart is a discovery platform, not a delivery service. We help customers find vendors, but transactions happen directly between customers and vendors.

How do I update my availability?

Simply log in to your vendor dashboard and toggle your availability status. It takes just one click!

Can I add photos to my profile?

Photo uploads are coming soon! For now, you can add detailed product descriptions to help customers find you.

Which cities are you available in?

We're currently expanding across major Indian cities. If your city isn't listed yet, sign up anyway - we're growing fast!

How can I partner with StreetKart?

We welcome partnerships with NGOs, community organizations, and local governments. Contact us at partnerships@streetkart.in
