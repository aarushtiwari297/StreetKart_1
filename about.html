Hyperlocal Bazaar Philosophy

Bridging the Digital Divide for Bharat's Street Vendors

StreetKart gives informal stalls digital permanence without expensive tech barriers, commissions, or complex logistics.

Our Mission

StreetKart exists to provide digital visibility to local street vendors who are often excluded from mainstream e-commerce platforms. We believe every vendor deserves the opportunity to be discovered by their local community.

The Problem We're Solving

For Vendors

  • ❌ Lack digital presence
  • ❌ Cannot signal daily availability
  • ❌ Excluded from platforms like Swiggy, Zomato
  • ❌ Miss out on potential customers

For Customers

  • ❌ Cannot reliably find nearby vendors
  • ❌ Don't know who is open
  • ❌ Miss local buying opportunities
  • ❌ Limited to formal retail options

Our Solution

Focused Approach

We solve visibility, not delivery. Simple, focused, and effective.

Low Barrier to Entry

Designed for vendors with low digital literacy. Easy signup, simple management.

Community Impact

Strengthening local commerce and supporting informal sector workers.

What StreetKart Is NOT

NOT a delivery platform — We focus on discovery, not logistics
NOT a payment gateway — Transactions happen directly with vendors
NOT an inventory system — Simple product listings, not complex management
